# 📈 Alpha Crypto Quant Portfolio: Dynamic Risk Management System

An institutional-grade quantitative trading algorithm that applies **Modern Portfolio Theory (Markowitz)** to the cryptocurrency market, enhanced with **Dynamic Rebalancing** and a **Macro Regime Filter** to strictly control maximum drawdowns.

## 🎯 The Business Problem
Standard Mean-Variance Optimization (Max Sharpe) mathematically fails during bear markets (especially in highly correlated markets like crypto). Static "buy-and-hold" portfolios suffer catastrophic drawdowns (-60% to -80%), triggering margin calls and unacceptable institutional risk. 
Furthermore, applying Markowitz to highly volatile assets with short historical lookback windows (90 days) often produces non-positive definite covariance matrices, leading to mathematical crashes and naive corner solutions.

## 💡 The Quant Solution
This system transforms academic theory into a robust algorithmic trading pipeline by combining three layers of risk management:

1. **Macro Regime Filter (Trend Following):** A binary on/off switch based on Bitcoin's 200-day Simple Moving Average (SMA 200). 
   * **Bull Market (Risk-ON):** The algorithm hunts for Alpha.
   * **Bear Market (Risk-OFF):** Markowitz is overridden. The portfolio executes a flight-to-safety, allocating 100% to Cash (USDC Stablecoin).
2. **Covariance Shrinkage (Ledoit-Wolf):** When Risk is ON, the model uses the *Ledoit-Wolf Shrinkage* method to denoise the covariance matrix. This prevents mathematical crashes and ensures the optimizer can efficiently allocate capital across highly volatile altcoins.
3. **Dynamic Rebalancing (Rolling Window):** Weights are not static. The algorithm looks back at the previous 90 days of volatility and dynamically recalculates the optimal allocations on the 1st of every month.

## 📊 The Algorithm in Action

### 1. In-Sample Phase (Learning)
* The model analyzes the previous 90 trading days.
* Applies Ledoit-Wolf Shrinkage to the Altcoin basket.
* Optimizes for the Maximum Sharpe Ratio.
* **Constraints:** Max 30% per individual asset to enforce strict diversification.

### 2. Out-of-Sample Phase (Backtest & Performance)
The algorithm was tested on a highly volatile Layer-1/AI crypto basket (`BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, TAO, CRO, XAVA, BNB`) from **2023 to 2026**.

**Key Metrics Achieved:**
* **Total Return:** Positive absolute return secured.
* **Max Drawdown:** Radically compressed (< 20%) compared to the standard crypto market baseline (-60%+).
